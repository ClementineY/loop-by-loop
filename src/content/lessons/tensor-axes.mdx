## Why should dimensions have names in your head?

PyTorch stores only sizes, not meanings. In `[batch, channels, height, width]`, dimension 1 means channels because the surrounding model agrees—not because the tensor knows.

## What does a reduction remove?

`mean`, `sum`, `max`, and similar operations combine values along one axis. Without `keepdim=True`, that axis disappears. Predict the output shape before running:

```python
images = torch.randn(8, 3, 32, 32)
channel_means = images.mean(dim=(0, 2, 3))
print(channel_means.shape)  # [3]
```

The batch, height, and width axes were reduced; one mean remains per channel.
